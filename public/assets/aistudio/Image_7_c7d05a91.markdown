## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-end flagship atmosphere tailored for advanced consumer hardware and pro-grade peripherals. It bridges the precision of technical architecture with the sensory curation of an executive showroom. Rather than relying on standard retail noise or saturated sale banners, the visual direction prioritizes spacious clarity, structural rigor, and deep chromatic focus.

The visual style merges Modern Minimalism with tactile, low-key depth:
- **Atmospheric Restraint:** Expansive neutral planes allow hardware silhouettes, display panels, and mechanical detailing to command primary visual attention.
- **Architectural Rhythm:** High-contrast geometric headlines set against technical body text communicate performance, industrial design heritage, and reliability.
- **Conversion-Driven Accents:** Key interaction points, purchase triggers, and promotional states leverage high-energy chromatic punctuations, cleanly isolated against quiet backdrops to accelerate decision-making without degrading brand prestige.

## Colors

The palette leverages deep structural tones to frame products while using vibrant accents exclusively for momentum and engagement.

- **Primary (`#101828`):** Deep Obsidian Navy. Acts as the anchor for structural framing, navigation bars, primary calls-to-action, high-contrast badges, and dominant typographic headers.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`):** Precision Cobalt. Employed for active navigational indicators, technical specification highlights, secondary buttons, interactive filters, and verified trust markers.
- **Tertiary (`#F97316`):** Radiant Amber. Strictly reserved for transactional urgency, conversion catalysts, pricing discounts, promotional tags, and limited-stock alerts.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`):** A stepped neutral ladder ensuring separation between global page canvas, product display cards, and inset specification matrices.
- **Typography Tokens:**
  - Ink High-Contrast (`#0F172A`): Core product titles, pricing integers, and primary headers.
  - Ink Medium (`#475569`): Technical descriptions, table labels, and inactive tabs.
  - Ink Subdued (`#94A3B8`): Strikethrough pricing, breadcrumb dividers, and placeholder states.
- **Structural Lines (`#E2E8F0`):** Subtle border delineations maintaining crisp grid divisions across high-density product comparisons.

## Typography

Typography balances commercial punch with industrial precision. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Manrope`):** Geometric, sculpted letterforms engineered for impactful product naming and hero narratives. Negative letter-spacing tightens visual gravity on large viewports.
- **Body & Data (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Highly legible grotesque structure suited for technical specification matrices, configuration options, dense reviews, and pricing metadata.
- **Price Architecture:** Price figures prioritize tabular alignment and optical prominence via `Manrope` in bold and extrabold weights. Currency symbols and decimal components step down in scale to anchor focus entirely on the core valuation.
- **Technical & Urgency Badges:** Rendered in uppercase with increased letter-spacing (`0.06em`) using `Hanken Grotesk` bold to ensure instant scannability on compact card viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is constructed on a 12-column fluid grid system that prioritizes structured modularity, consistent gutter rhythm, and distinct sectional breathing space.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max-width capped at 1440px to retain luxury store containment.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, `margin-tablet: 2rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Product cards default to 2 or 3 columns.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-sm: 1rem`. Product listings reflow to 2 columns with reduced internal padding.
- **Spacing Scale Application:**
  - `space-xs` (4px): Inset badge margins, rating stars separation, and form field helper spacing.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Gaps between specification tags, colorway chips, and inline price variants.
  - `space-md` (16px): Internal padding for card content, default button vertical spacing, input box heights.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Card container internal padding, stack gaps between major module headers and content.
  - `space-xl` (40px): Section divisions, product gallery thumbnail stacks, and drawer margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of tonal layering and diffused ambient shadows rather than harsh drop shadows. This recreates the physical sensation of premium electronics resting on clean exhibition plinths.

- **Flat Surface (Level 0):** Used for base backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`). Pure flush alignment with subtle 1px border lines in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Card Rest State (Level 1):** Background `#FFFFFF` enveloped by a composite boundary: a fine keyline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) reinforced with an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.03)`.
- **Card Hover & Active Components (Level 2):** Elevated interactive state. Border darkens subtly to `#CBD5E1`, with shadow widening to `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.03)`. Product imagery slightly scales within this elevation tier.
- **Floating Controls & Modals (Level 3):** Fixed purchase bars, cart drawers, and quick-view panels. Layered with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04)`.
- **Specular Backdrops:** Frosted navigation headers apply `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a 90% opaque `#FFFFFF` or `#101828` base to retain spatial continuity during scroll.

## Shapes

The shape geometry aligns with modern high-precision tech hardware: crisp, controlled, and balanced. Roundedness level `2` sets the standard base at 8px (`0.5rem`).

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Form fields, standard primary and secondary buttons, filter chips, rating badges, and dropdown menus.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px):** Catalog product cards, review summary blocks, media display panels, and checkout card wrappers.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl` - 1.5rem / 24px):** Hero promotional containers, featured product showcases, and modal dialog shells.
- **Pill Exception (`rounded-full`):** Exclusively designated for floating discount stickers, status indicator dots, and thumbnail swatch selectors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Buy Now):** Background `#101828`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, height 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`, border-radius 8px. Font `Hanken Grotesk` 15px weight 600. On hover, background shifts to `#1E293B` with Level 2 elevation.
- **Conversion / Promotional Action:** Background `#F97316`, foreground `#FFFFFF`. Used for single-click checkouts and limited drops. Hover shifts to `#EA580C`.
- **Secondary / Technical:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, foreground `#0F172A`. On hover, subtle tint to `#F1F5F9`.

### Badges & Tags
- **Discount & Promo Badges:** Solid `#F97316` background with crisp white typography, or soft variant featuring `#FFF7ED` fill with `#C2410C` text and 1px `#FED7AA` border. Pill-shaped or 4px radius.
- **Technical Specs Badges:** Inset `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text, uppercase tracking. Perfect for processor details, RAM size, or connectivity protocols.

### Product Cards
- **Architecture:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` card framed by a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and `rounded-lg` corners.
- **Top Zone:** Aspect ratio 1:1 neutral gray container (`#F8FAFC`) with floating badge anchors (top left) and wishlist icon toggle (top right).
- **Body Zone:** Brand title (`label-md` in `#94A3B8`), product name (`headline-sm` in `#0F172A`), spec pills row (`space-xs` gap), star rating row with numerical count.
- **Bottom Zone:** Old price (`body-sm` strikethrough in `#94A3B8`), active price (`price-card` in `#0F172A`), and rapid cart add button (`#101828` or `#2563EB` icon-only variant).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 44px height, 1px `#E2E8F0` boundary, radius 8px. Text typed in `#0F172A`, placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border `#2563EB` backed by a 3px diffused outer halo `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px boxes, filled with `#2563EB` upon selection with an interior white checkmark. Inactive state features a `#CBD5E1` contour.

### Lists & Key-Value Spec Tables
- **Layout:** Alternating `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC` row fills or clean divider lines (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Typography:** Left key label in `Hanken Grotesk` medium (`#475569`), right value in bold `#0F172A`.

### Hardware Swatches & Filters
- **Colorway Pickers:** Circular 24px swatches with an internal color pill wrapped in a 2px offset white ring, enclosed by an outer `#101828` active ring when selected.
